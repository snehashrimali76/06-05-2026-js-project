Simple Tools for a Healthier Life

Saral Sahara provides three essential services tailored specifically for the needs of rural communities.

Medicine Reminders

"Never miss a dose." Our service allows users to easily register their medication schedules. We then send automated, timely alerts directly to their mobile device in their local language.

This is crucial for managing long-term conditions like diabetes or blood pressure, where consistency is key to health.

Child Vaccination Log

"Protect your child's future." Mothers can easily maintain a secure, digital record of their children's immunization history. The log also provides alerts for upcoming mandatory vaccines.

This ensures complete compliance with child health protocols and simplifies record-keeping for every parent.

Local Language Medical Help

"Clear communication is safe communication." We provide access to medical advice and platform support in multiple local languages.

This removes the critical barrier of language, ensuring that users fully understand their health conditions, prescriptions, and necessary actions.
